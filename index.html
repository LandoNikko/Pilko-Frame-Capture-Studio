Pilko Studio

Pilko Studio is a local web tool for capturing screenshots from video files privately in many different ways.

Extraction Modes

  • Manual
    Capture by hand from the timeline.
  • Interval
    Captures by time interval, video frame interval or fixed count.
  • Automatic
    Captures are based on changes in content.
    • Histogram Diff compares the overall distribution of colors and brightness between frames to detect and capture scene changes.
    • Pixel Diff compares individual pixels between frames and measures how much of the image has changed to detect and capture scene changes.
    • Transcription captures from timestamped segments. Requires matching SRT or VTT file.
  • Grid
    Combine captures into a single grid image.

Features

  • Compatible on any browser - computer to phone.
  • Content overlay support (Timecode, SMPTE/EBU, text and image overlays) for professional workflows.
  • Automatic framerate detection.
  • Crop video area and set time range capture.
  • Automated and manual capturing.
  • Fine-tune every capture by time and frame.
  • Configure capture quality (PNG, JPEG, WEBP).
  • Extensive filename output configuration.
  • Save individually or all at once.

Processing & Privacy

All video processing and screenshot captures happen within the browser: NO sign-up, NO subscription, NO cookies, NO installations, NO cloud processing, NO AI.
